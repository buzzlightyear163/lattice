§1

Your address is your public key.

  1. 1.1

    A Solana address is 32 bytes written in base58. On Bitcoin, an unused address hides your key behind a hash. On Solana there is no hash. The address is the key.

  2. 1.2

    That key is a point A on Curve25519: a fixed point B, multiplied by your secret number a. Multiplying forward takes microseconds. Walking back from A to a takes a classical computer about 2128 steps.

  3. 1.3

    Shor’s algorithm walks it back. Google Quantum AI’s 2026 estimate for a 256-bit curve: under 1,200 logical qubits, under half a million physical qubits, minutes of runtime.Babbush et al., 2026

  4. 1.4

    Lattices have no such shortcut. ML-DSA and Falcon hide the secret as a short vector in a lattice of more than a thousand dimensions, and no known quantum algorithm finds it efficiently. That is the key LATTICE forges.

The road to Q-day.

          
Nobody knows the date. Here is how close the parts already are.

        

      

      
    
        
  1. 1994
    Peter Shor publishes a quantum algorithm for factoring and discrete logarithms, the problems behind RSA and elliptic curves.
    2. 
        
  2. Mar 2020
    Solana’s mainnet beta goes live. Every wallet address is an Ed25519 public key.
    3. 
        
  3. Aug 2024
    NIST publishes FIPS 203, 204 and 205. ML-DSA becomes the standard post-quantum signature.
    4. 
        
  4. Nov 2024
    NIST’s draft transition plan: quantum-vulnerable signatures such as ECDSA deprecated after 2030, disallowed after 2035.
    5. 
        
  5. Dec 2024
    Google’s Willow chip shows error rates falling as its qubit grid grows, a key threshold for error correction.
    6. 
        
  6. Jan 2025
    The Solana Winternitz Vault ships: an opt-in, hash-based vault for quantum-resistant storage.
    7. 
        
  7. Dec 2025
    Project Eleven and the Solana Foundation run a Solana testnet with post-quantum signatures.
    8. 
        
  8. Mar 2026
    Google Quantum AI: a 256-bit elliptic curve could fall to under 1,200 logical qubits, in minutes.
    9. 
        
  9. Apr 2026
    Anza and Firedancer each implement Falcon signatures for Solana, ready if the threat becomes credible.
    10. 
        
  10. Oct 2026
    LATTICE: forge a post-quantum key for your Solana wallet, in your browser.
    11. 
        
  11. Q-day
    Not on any calendar yet. Your key can be ready before it is.
